Perception IT — Internal Self‑Assessment Checklist

Self‑assessment guide only. Not a substitute for professional engineering evaluation.

  • Facility & room data
    Room dimensions, ceiling height, floor type, door locations
  • Equipment schedule
    List of racks, servers, nameplate power, PSU counts
  • Cooling plant summary
    CRAC/CRAH types, chiller specs, redundancy and N+ configuration
  • Airflow paths
    Open/blocked perforated tiles, return/exhaust routes, containment presence
  • Temperature & humidity targets
    Ambient setpoints, allowable ΔT, alarms and setpoint ranges
  • Sensing & telemetry
    Rack-level sensors, BMS points, logging frequency
  • Power & UPS
    UPS capacity, transfer times, maintenance bypass plan
  • Monsoon / dust hardening
    Drain sizing, filtration, seals, humidity mitigation
  • Operational procedures
    Maintenance cadence, alarm response, load testing schedule
  • Fallback & redundancy
    Spare parts, failover plan, vendor SLAs for replacement

Use this checklist to capture inputs for a remote thermal review. Where physical verification is required, engage a local commissioning partner.
