# Assessment Section — Image Concept Brief

## Section Context
Placed in the **Assessment** section ("Your Two Assessment Options"), to the right of the heading text. The section sits on a **white background** (`#FFFFFF`).

## Critical Requirement
**Background must be pure white (`#FFFFFF`).** The image should blend seamlessly into the section background with no visible edges, borders, or boxing. It should feel like the illustration is part of the page, not an asset placed on top of it.

---

## Concept: Thermal Assessment in Progress

An isometric illustration showing a **thermal assessment being conducted in a data centre environment**.

### Core Subject
- **Single technician** in professional attire (no hard hat — this is an assessment, not construction)
- Holding a **thermal imaging camera** or tablet showing heat-map readouts
- Standing beside **1–2 server racks** with visible inlet temperature displays
- A **floating checklist/clipboard** nearby showing assessment criteria

### Supporting Elements
- **Temperature readouts** on racks: `24.5°C`, `27.1°C`, `22.8°C` (mixed green/orange values — some good, some flagged)
- **Heat gradient overlay** on one rack section — subtle warm tones showing a hotspot
- **Small data panel** floating nearby with a simple bar graph or scorecard
- **One copper/bronze heat sink** visible inside an open rack section

### Colour Palette (Muted Professional)
| Element | Colour | Usage |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Base Blue | `#4A6FA9` → `#6B8CC4` | Server racks, technician clothing |
| Cool Gray | `#8B9FB3` → `#A8B5C9` | Transparency layers, equipment |
| Data Cyan | `#6FB8C9` → `#8FD4E0` | Temperature readouts, metrics |
| Warm Accent | `#D4947A` → `#C9826A` | Heat indicators, flagged temperatures |
| Vibrant Cyan | `#00E5FF` | Sparingly — active sensor glow |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | **Pure white — seamless blend** |

### Material Language
- **Frosted glass panels** for floating data displays (60–80% opacity)
- **Brushed aluminum** server rack frames
- **Soft light diffusion** — no harsh shadows
- **Edge highlighting** with thin cyan lines on glass panels

### Composition
- **Isometric view** (30-degree angle)
- **Primary subject** on the left/center: technician + racks
- **Floating elements** on the right: checklist, data panel, temperature readouts
- **Negative space** on the right edge — leaves room for web layout breathing room
- **Depth of field** — foreground sharp, background softly blurred

### What NOT to Include
- No dark backgrounds or gradients
- No boxes, frames, or container edges
- No text labels or logos on the image itself
- No dramatic lighting or shadows
- No people other than the single technician

---

## Emotional Tone
- **Precise** — clean lines, technical accuracy
- **Cool but alert** — calm blue tones with selective warm accents for flagged issues
- **Professional** — enterprise-grade quality, not dramatic
